# Beta Feedback Form

## Session Setup

1. Which positioning variant best matches why you tried DropSquash today?
2. Which build under test did you use?
3. About how many files did you try to compress in this session?

## Intake

4. What kind of recording did you compress today?
5. What were you trying to do after compressing it?
6. Roughly how often do you make Mac screen recordings each week?

## Outcome

7. Did DropSquash finish all files you wanted to convert?
8. How many files finished successfully?
9. Did you complete a real 3-file session today?
10. Did the output become small enough for the job you had in mind?
11. Did you keep the original, move it to Trash, or ignore that choice?

## Friction

12. What was confusing or slow?
13. Did you hesitate because of privacy, quality, or trust?
14. If you stopped before three successful files, what prevented you from doing more?

## Value

15. If DropSquash disappeared tomorrow, what would you do instead?
16. Would you use this again on another day for the same job?
17. Would you pay for this once it feels reliable enough?
18. What price would feel fair for a local Mac utility that solves this job?
19. If paid beta checkout were available today, would you buy it now?

## Follow-up

20. May we contact you for a 15-minute follow-up interview?
21. What email or handle should we use?
