# Phase 10 Decision Package

## Purpose

This is the owner decision record for a limited launch. It is not a checklist
that turns missing proof into a sale. Complete it only with versioned evidence
from Phases 6 through 9.

## Decision Record

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Decision date | Pending |
| Candidate build and commit | Pending |
| Lead lane | Pending: C2C Pro or B2B Secure Share |
| Decision | Pending: Go, Iterate, or Stop |
| Owner approval | Required before any paid or public release |

## Non-Negotiable Evidence

| Gate | C2C Pro evidence | B2B Secure Share evidence | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product truth | Safe conversion, trial, license, and original-safety results | Bounded native capture, destructive output, and independent decoder evidence | In progress |
| Fixture evidence | Not applicable to conversion claim | Versioned fixture matrix with every result visible | In progress |
| Unsafe publication | Zero published outputs that missed a required check | Zero published outputs that missed a required check | Pending packaged run |
| Distribution | Exact signed, notarized artifact and clean-machine result | Same for each offered platform | macOS notarization pending; Windows unavailable |
| Demand | Three conversions and repeat use per active tester | One representative reviewed workflow per QA lead | No cohort evidence |
| Commercial surface | Price, support, refund, terms, and license match the build | Pilot scope, support, retention, and exclusions match the build | Deferred |

## Go Rules

Choose `Go` only when one lane independently satisfies every applicable row.

- C2C Go: conversion reliability, trial accounting, original safety,
  notarized macOS artifact, and private cohort demand are demonstrated.
- B2B Go: a bounded fixture matrix, residual-free decoded outcomes for every
  advertised row, explicit fail-closed exclusions, signed artifact, named
  pilot scope, and QA-lead workflow evidence are demonstrated.
- No `Go` decision enables a new platform, selective claim, checkout, or
  public page beyond the evidence reviewed here.

## Iterate Rules

Choose `Iterate` when the product is technically promising but one or more
required rows are incomplete without a safety incident. Record a maximum of
three next experiments, an owner, and an expiry date. Do not market the missing
capability while iterating.

## Stop Rules

Choose `Stop` or pause the affected lane when any of these occurs:

- an accepted artifact exposes a synthetic required region after independent
  decode;
- a required continuity, writer, receipt, or verifier check can be bypassed;
- support capacity, retention, or willingness to pay fails the stated pilot
  threshold; or
- release copy, policy, price, or distribution evidence disagrees with the
  actual build.

Stopping B2B Secure Share does not require stopping C2C Pro, and vice versa.

## Evidence Attachments

Record only redacted, reproducible references:

- fixture ID and immutable version;
- app version, commit, platform, and native bridge version;
- final MP4 SHA-256, signed plan or receipt verification result, and decoder
  verification result;
- failure classification for every rejected run;
- cohort counts, consented feedback summary, price anchor, and support hours.

Never attach raw captured frames, recognized text, customer identifiers,
window titles, application names, or credentials.
