# Beta Interview Script

## Setup

1. Which positioning variant best describes why you tried DropSquash?
2. Which build under test did you use?
3. Did you complete one real 3-file session before this interview?

## Opening

4. Thanks for trying DropSquash.
5. We are testing whether it solves a real Mac screen-recording problem.
6. We are not testing you. We want to understand the job and the friction.

## Workflow

7. Tell me about the last time you made a screen recording.
8. What happened between recording it and sharing or storing it?
9. Where did file size become a problem?

## Product Fit

10. What made you try DropSquash instead of doing nothing?
11. What part felt immediately useful?
12. What part made you suspicious or hesitant?

## Repetition

13. Would you use this again the next time you record your screen?
14. In what situation would you definitely come back to it on another day?
15. In what situation would you skip it?
16. What would need to happen for DropSquash to become part of a repeat workflow?

## Payment

17. If DropSquash were stable and signed, would you pay for it?
18. What outcome would need to feel guaranteed before paying?
19. What price range feels believable for that guarantee?
20. If paid beta checkout existed today, would you buy now, later, or not at all?

## Closing

21. Should this stay focused on screen recordings, or is there a nearby job you
    wish it handled too?
22. What is the one thing we should improve before asking more people to pay?
