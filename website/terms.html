Terms of Service

These draft terms describe the intended rules for using the DropSquash paid beta before public checkout goes live.

Product scope

DropSquash is currently a local-first macOS app for turning supported screen recordings into smaller MP4 files. Current public support is limited to the implemented paid-beta scope.

License and activation

Paid use depends on a valid license and activation flow. Additional license details are described in the License policy.

You remain responsible for the media you process, the destinations where you share outputs, and verifying that a compressed file still fits your workflow before replacing any manual process.
