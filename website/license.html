License policy

A personal Pro license unlocks unlimited successful conversions on an activated device.

Unlock Pro once to keep squashing on that Mac. Your recordings stay local.

The app stores a license-key fingerprint, instance identifiers, and validation timestamps. It does not persist the raw license key.

The desktop forget action removes the local license cache on that Mac. Server-side deactivation requires the raw license key and is not automatic.

Offline grace is allowed after a successful validation. If grace expires, reconnect once to refresh Pro on that Mac.

This page does not replace the Terms of Service. For activation trouble, use support. Refund handling is described in the refund policy draft.
