License policy

A personal Pro license unlocks unlimited successful conversions on an activated device.

The app stores a license-key fingerprint, instance identifiers, and validation timestamps. It does not persist the raw license key.

The desktop forget action clears the local license cache on that Mac. Server-side deactivation requires the raw license key and is not automatic.

Offline grace is allowed after a successful validation. If grace expires, reconnect once to refresh your license.
