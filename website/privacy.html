DropSquash

Privacy

  • Screen recording conversion runs locally on your computer.
  • DropSquash does not upload media for compression.
  • Telemetry is off by default.
  • License activation contacts Lemon Squeezy only when you enter a license key.
  • The updater is disabled for the first paid beta.
  • Original files are kept unless a verified conversion and your chosen policy allow Trash.
  • Optional privacy receipts are local JSON files that record uploaded_bytes = 0, metadata_policy = preserve, and file names instead of absolute paths.
