What it does

DropSquash is a small desktop app for post-processing Mac screen recordings before you share them in chat, docs, or issue trackers. The current product scope is intentionally narrow: one recording in, one smaller MP4 out.

What it does not do

It does not upload your media for conversion, run command-line media tools in the product path, include built-in screen recording or automatic privacy masking in the current beta, promise unsupported Windows or Linux conversion yet, or delete originals by default.

Local first

Compression runs on your Mac. Media is not uploaded for conversion.

Original safe

Originals stay put unless you explicitly move them to Trash after a verified conversion.

Made for sharing

Profiles stay focused on common chat, docs, and archive workflows without adding cloud dependency.

Before public release

DropSquash is being productized in a strict order: public product information, legal pages, pricing expectations, and distribution readiness must match the real app before checkout goes live. The paid beta is still gated on Developer ID signing, notarization, checksum publication, and release blocker verification.
