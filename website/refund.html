Refund policy

This draft policy covers the pre-checkout paid beta until public checkout goes live.

DropSquash is currently sold as a narrow local Mac utility for shrinking screen recordings safely.

Product-use rules are described separately on the Terms of Service page.

If DropSquash cannot activate or cannot perform its basic local conversion workflow on a supported Mac, contact support with your order email and app version.

During the private or manual paid beta, refund requests are handled manually after support review.

After public checkout goes live, refund requests are reviewed through the Lemon Squeezy order flow for the order used to buy the license.
