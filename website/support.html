FAQ

Does DropSquash upload my videos?

No. Video compression runs locally on your computer.

Does it delete originals?

No. Originals are kept by default. Cleanup only moves originals to Trash after a successful verified conversion.

Does a failed conversion count?

No. Failed or cancelled conversions do not count toward the 10-conversion trial.

Does it use ffmpeg?

No. The product media path does not shell out to ffmpeg or ffprobe.
